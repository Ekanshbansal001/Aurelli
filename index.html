Our Story

Crafted to last beyond the moment.

AURELIA began with a simple frustration: too much fine jewellery is designed to be photographed once and worn rarely after. We set out to make the opposite — pieces built for the version of your life that actually happens, not just the occasion.

Every design passes through the same discipline before it ever reaches a client: a form simplified until nothing more can be removed, a setting engineered to survive years of ordinary wear, and a finish that ages the way good gold should — softly, not shabbily.

We work in small batches with a limited number of skilled hands, which means slower production and, we think, a better piece at the end of it.

The Art of the Detail

What Sets a Piece Apart

01 Thoughtful Design

Every piece starts as a question about how it will actually be worn — with a coat sleeve, through a full day at a desk, layered against skin — before it's ever a question of how it will look in a single photograph.

02 Fine Craftsmanship

Settings are finished and checked by hand, not just cast and shipped. A piece isn't released until it has survived the same wear test we'd expect a client to put it through in its first year.

03 Carefully Selected Materials

We work almost exclusively in 18K gold for its balance of durability and colour, and every stone is hand-selected for how it behaves in ordinary light, not just under a jeweller's loupe.

04 Made to Be Remembered

We'd rather make fewer pieces that mean something than more pieces that don't. If a design doesn't earn a place in someone's everyday rotation, it doesn't stay in the edit for long.
